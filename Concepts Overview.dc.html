02

How to read these concepts

What a concept is

A look and feel exploration only, testing how we can apply the brand digitally. Each is shown on the homepage simply because it's the front door. Content is TBC: copy, imagery and statistics are placeholders.

Things to think about

Does it represent the Winchester personality? Do your first impressions align with who you are?

What happens next

Kel's wireframes set the content structure and IA. Once we agree a single direction, we apply it to the wireframes and build out the design system.

A note on scope. These concepts purely explore creative direction, so some ideas may or may not be in scope. We'd rather push the ideas first, then pull them back.

03

Three concepts. One brand, in the making.

Concept AStepping through

This route explores the motifs as a mask, used subtly. It leads with film, revealed through an arch you step through on load.

Built around student stories, with the arch carrying you into each one.

Concept BPushing the brand

This route uses the motifs as shapes on screen, baked into the UI. They frame the AI search and act as windows into student stories.

Dark ground, lime for interaction and big animated type.

Concept CPared back

The quietest route, relying on layout over motion.

Mostly sans serif, with motif curves as section dividers.
